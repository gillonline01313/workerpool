	 Apply for Community Recommendation 
 Note: Designation does not guarantee that a candidate will receive a community recommendation or permanent residence. Every job offer and candidate must undergo a full review by both the West Kootenay RCIP (community level) and Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (federal level). 
 If approved, you’ll receive designation status and can begin submitting Recommendation Applications for candidates you&#8217;d like to hire. 
 You must submit accurate and complete recommendation applications for each candidate.&nbsp;
