        Privacy

        Your documents are read, never kept

        Shrunk in your browser

        Before anything leaves your device, your browser turns each file into a small image and removes photo location data. The original file never leaves your device.

        Read in memory

        Each image goes over an encrypted connection to an AI reader running on Cloudflare Workers AI. It's held in memory only while it's read. Cloudflare doesn't keep it after the answer is sent and doesn't use it to train AI.

        Nothing written down

        We don't save your files, the text in them or your results in any database, storage bucket, log or email. Close or reset the page and your results are gone too. We keep only your pass (an ID, its dates and how many documents it has read) and an anonymous count of scans to stop abuse. Payments are handled by Stripe; we never see your card.

        Which documents give the most accurate score?

        Your language test result, your passport (for your age), your education documents with your ECA, and reference letters that show dates, hours a week, duties and pay. Pay stubs and T4s help confirm Canadian work. If you're married or common-law, your partner's language test and education help too.

        Why is my score different from what IRCC shows?

        The scanner can misread a document, and your Express Entry profile counts only what you can prove. Check every field in step 2. Experience gained while you were a full-time student, self-employment in Canada, and jobs in TEER 4 or 5 occupations don't count for Express Entry work points.

        Which rules does the score use?

        By default, the rules IRCC announced for November 3, 2026, where Canadian work experience and a high-wage job offer share up to 150 points. You can switch to the rules in force until November 2, 2026 on the results.

        Is this immigration advice?

        No. WorkerPool is a data processing company. The scanner gives an estimate from public IRCC rules. For advice about your situation, speak with a licensed immigration consultant (CICC) or a lawyer.
