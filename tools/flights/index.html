How it finds what others miss

Five ways to pay less, checked in one search

Self-transfer through a hub

Two separate tickets, for example Edmonton → London and London → Delhi, can cost far less than one ticket. Most search sites only sell routes one airline group sells together, so they never show these.

Two one-ways vs a return

Sometimes the cheapest outbound and the cheapest return are on different airlines. The tool compares both and tells you which is cheaper.

Cheaper days and airports

A price calendar for the whole month, the cheapest days within 3 days of yours, and nearby airports (Calgary instead of Edmonton, say) when they save you money.

Price alerts

Set a target price or let the tool decide what a very good deal is. It re-checks every few hours and tells you by email, Telegram or a notification on your phone, including in the WorkerPool Android app.

Where prices come from

Fares come from Aviasales, which compares hundreds of airlines and travel agencies. Shown prices are recent fares found in the last 48 hours; the booking page always shows the live price before you pay.

Self-transfer: know the risks

On separate tickets, if your first flight is late and you miss the second, the second airline doesn't have to rebook you. You also collect and re-check your bags and may need a transit visa for the hub country. The tool leaves at least 3 hours to connect.
