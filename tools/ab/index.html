Before you start

This is not legal or immigration advice.Alberta Pathway Check is a free, self-interactive tool. It shows roughly where a person stands across Express Entry, the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) and the Rural Community Immigration Pilot, based only on the details you enter.
  • Scores are tentative estimates. Official scores are set by IRCC, the Alberta Advantage Immigration Program (AAIP) and each community after reviewing documents.
  • Program rules and draw results change often. Always check official government sources before acting.
  • For advice on a specific case, speak with a licensed immigration consultant (CICC) or lawyer.
  • WorkerPool is a data processing company, not an immigration representative.

This tool gives a tentative estimate for general information only. It is not immigration advice and doesn't guarantee an invitation, nomination or visa. Scores depend on documents that IRCC and the Government of Alberta verify. Express Entry scoring follows IRCC's Comprehensive Ranking System, including the changes announced for November 3, 2026; the full revised grid had not been published by IRCC at the time of writing, so the high-wage parts are estimates. AAIP scoring follows the Alberta Worker Expression of Interest points grid; stream requirements are simplified. Claresholm doesn't publish its RCIP points grid, so community points are estimates. Proof-of-funds amounts are IRCC's July 2025 figures. Draw data: Express Entry to October 16, 2026; AAIP to October 20, 2026.
