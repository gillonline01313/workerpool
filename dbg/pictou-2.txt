 Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP)    
 The Pictou County Partnership, the leading economic development organization for the Pictou County region, is proud to be one of 14 selected communities participating in the Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP). This innovative program supports rural workforce growth by assisting local employers in filling critical roles that align with our region’s labour needs and economic opportunities.
 RCIP is a permanent resident pathway that not only helps employers recruit international talent but also prioritizes the success of newcomers who choose to build their futures in Pictou County. The Partnership is dedicated to supporting successful applicants through our ‘community hug’ approach, connecting them with settlement services and community integration initiatives to ensure they settle, thrive, and contribute to the vibrant growth of our region.
 How RCIP Benefits Employers :
 Local Consultation Phase : Local employers are invited to share their labour needs, helping inform the annual RCIP priority sectors and occupations.
 Employer-Driven : Employers actively participate in selecting and recommending candidates who align with their business needs.
 Attract & Retain Talent : RCIP provides a pathway to permanent residency, supporting businesses in building diverse, skilled teams for long-term success.
 Community Integration : The Partnership works closely with local settlement service providers to ensure newcomers are fully supported as they integrate into the community.
 RCIP empowers Pictou County employers to attract and retain international talent to fill labour shortages that cannot be met locally, helping businesses thrive and strengthen our region’s workforce.
 For more details on the application process and eligibility, visit our dedicated RCIP website at Pictou County RCIP .
