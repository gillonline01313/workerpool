 CEDC Immigration Pilots
 2026 Employer-Led Rural Community Immigration Pilot Program is now Open
 The Thunder Bay Community Economic Development Commission (CEDC) has officially launched the second year of the Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP), an employer-driven initiative designed to strengthen workforce attraction and retention by providing a streamlined immigration pathway for skilled workers.

 Regional Economic Development through Immigration (REDI) initiative
 The CEDC is pleased to announce it has successfully secured the Thunder Bay District Census Division’s participation in the 2026–2027 Regional Economic Development through Immigration (REDI) initiative, delivered through Ontario’s Immigrant Nominee Program (OINP).
 Designed to help regional communities attract and retain skilled international talent, the initiative will provide the Thunder Bay District with an expected allocation of 150 immigration nominations in 2027. This will create additional opportunities for local employers to address critical workforce shortages while providing eligible foreign workers with a pathway to permanent residence.
 Local employers looking to fill or retain skilled positions can learn more by clicking this link:  Regional Economic Development through Immigration (REDI) initiative | ontario.ca
