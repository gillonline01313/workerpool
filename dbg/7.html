Overview

Immigration New Brunswick:

  • invites candidates in the New Brunswick Provincial Nominee Program candidate pool to submit complete applications
  • selects complete applications in the Atlantic Immigration Program candidate pool for processing

Invitations and selections are based on provincial labour market needs, available allocation and other priorities determined by the Government of New Brunswick.
