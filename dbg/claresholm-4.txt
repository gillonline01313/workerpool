 The Centennial Park Campground
 Escape The Everyday
 4604 - 4th Street West 
 Located in Centennial Park ( View Map ), this seasonal campground offers full-service camping, with hook-ups for water, sewer, and 30AMP services. A new shower building was recently constructed that offers change room areas, showers, and a camp kitchen for picnics. Free WiFi, horseshoe pits, ball diamonds, a playground and the Spray Park are also available for your enjoyment! The campground is within walking distance of the downtown, Bridges at Claresholm Golf Course, and the Claresholm Aquatic Centre. Sani-dump is also available at no charge. 
 *NEW* - RESERVATIONS 
 The Centennial Park Campground uses an online reservation system. To book your stay, please visit  Campspot - Centennial Park . It's easy and quick and you can make payment directly for your stay via credit, debit, or e-transfer.

 Land Acknowledgment
 The Town of Claresholm respectfully acknowledges that we are located on the ancestral and traditional
territory of the Blackfoot Confederacy including the Kainai, Piikani, and Siksika Nations, as well as the
Tsuut’ina Nation, the Stoney Nakoda Nations, Ktunaxa Nation and Metis Nations. We honour and respect
their culture, traditions, teachings, and enduring relationship with these lands and waters. We provide this
recognition as an act of reconciliation and gratitude.
