 IMMIGRATION
 Rural Community Immigration Pilot
 The Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP) is a new federal economic immigration program. It helps eligible rural businesses hire skilled foreign workers from outside of Canada or support current temporary foreign workers in their efforts to gain permanent residency.
 Approved foreign workers and their dependent family members can apply for permanent residency to live and work in Altona/Rhineland long-term. This helps build a stronger local workforce and allows newcomers to establish stable, fulfilling lives in our community.
 How to Become a Designated Employer
 Follow the steps below to become a designated employer under the Altona/Rhineland program
 1. Meet with the Economic Development Officer (EDO) Schedule a meeting with the EDO to confirm that:
 Your business falls within one of Altona/Rhineland’s approved priority industries.
 The position you are hiring for is on the approved list of occupations.
 2. Complete Required Training 
 IRCC Employer Onboarding Training
 IRCC’s Building Welcoming Workplaces Training
 3. Submit the Employer Designation Application 
 Complete and submit the official Employer Designation Application form.
 4. Receive Designation Confirmation 
 Once the steps above are completed and your application is approved, the EDO will issue an official designation confirmation letter.
 5. Get Listed as a Designated Employer 
 Your business will be added to the public list of designated employers on our website.
 6. Submit Candidate Recommendations 
 As a designated employer, you may now submit applications for recommendations on behalf of your candidates.
 For Foreign Nationals Interested in Working in Altona/Rhineland
 If you are a foreign national interested in the Rural Community Immigration Pilot (RCIP), click here to view current employment opportunities in Altona/Rhineland.
 HOW TO APPLY
 Only apply for jobs that match your skills and experience.
 Carefully read the job postings and make sure you meet all mandatory requirements before submitting your application.
 One active application at a time.
 You may only have one active job application at any time. An active application is any job you’ve applied for through your Candidate Profile.
 You can withdraw your application
 If you find another job that is a better fit for your qualifications.
 Don’t See a Suitable Job Right Now?
 If no current job postings match your background, you can still [create a Candidate Profile]. This will allow you to:
 Set up career alerts that notify you by email when a job matches your criteria.
 What You Need to Apply
 A complete resume is required when applying. Please include the following: 
 Your full name and contact information
 Your education history
 Your work history
 (Optional) Skills and activities
 Three references (preferably work-related)
