 The Superior East region includes the communities of Chapleau , Dubreuilville , Wawa , White River , Hornepayne, and Manitouwadge, and surrounding First Nations.
 Francophone Community Immigration Pilot General Information
 The Superior East region was selected as one of six regions across Canada to host the Francophone Community Immigration Pilot (FCIP), a federal initiative aimed at supporting the attraction and retention of French-speaking newcomers to enhance the region’s economic development and cultural vitality.
