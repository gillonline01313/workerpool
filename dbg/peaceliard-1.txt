 What is the Rural Community Immigration Pilot? 
 The Rural Community Immigration Pilot offers permanent residence (PR) to skilled workers who want to work and settle in rural and more remote communities.
 ​ 
 Immigration, Refugees & Citizenship Canada chose 14 communities who showed they can support and benefit from skilled migrants. They will designate employers in priority sectors to hire for jobs that they can&rsquo;t fill with local workers.
 ​ 
 The Northeast BC region has partnered with Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) to develop a program that works best for our community by prioritizing skilled immigrants who intend to make Northeast BC their home.
 For more information, please visit: Rural Community Immigration Pilot .
 Our Region: Northeast BC – Peace Liard 
 The Peace Liard region, located in the northeast corner of British Columbia, is a vast and diverse area made up of two regional jurisdictions: 
 the Peace River Regional District (PRRD) 
 the Northern Rockies Regional Municipality (NRRM). 
 Together, these two areas form what is commonly referred to as the Peace Liard region.
 Our region offers skilled workers the opportunity to build a life in welcoming, community-oriented towns and rural areas. Our region is rich in natural resources, industry, and culture, and is known for its breathtaking landscapes, strong local economies, and supportive communities.
