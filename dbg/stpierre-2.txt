 RV Campground
 R.V. Campground
 STAY AND PLAY 2026 
 2 FOR 1 GOLF AT MAPLEWOOD G.C.
 VALID MON-FRI...EXCL. HOLIDAYS
 PLUS 
 Enjoy these offers at local businesses:
 -Lucky Luc's Bar & Grill
 -St. Pierre Bigway
 -St. Pierre Bakery
 -Le Bebe Rouge Restaurant
 OFFER ONLY VALID WITH A MINIMUM 2 NIGHT STAY 
 Expires Oct. 31/26
 RV Campground 
 2025 Season Rates 
 Fully Serviced Lots Sites 1-7 $27.50/night 
 Electrical Only Lots Sites 8-13 $22.50/night 
 Tent Only Site 14 $15.00/night 
 Monthly Site Rental (Full Hook Up) Sites 1 & 4 $400/month 
 Everything necessary for a comfortable stay is available. (See Parc Carillon in this section as well as the Photo Gallery for more information about the park.)
 Rules & Regulations of the Parc Carillon Overnight Campground 
 Quiet hours are 11 pm to 7 am
 Keep campsites clean
 Campfires are to be kept inside of fire pits only
 Pets are to remain leashed at all times
 Personal Property/Liability 
 The Parc Carillon assumes no responsibility for injury or accident to campers or guests. All campers & guests should take normal precaution against theft. Please lock up any personal property or valuables when not on the premises.
 Terms of Condition, Cancellation and refund policy can be found on our booking site.
