    Your documents

    Read once. Then destroyed.

    Your phone or computer shrinks each file into a few page images. Our server reads them in memory to fill in your profile and throws them away as soon as the answer is sent back. No document, photo, file name or page text is written to a database, file storage, log or email. The page then clears the files from your browser as well. What we keep is only the profile you confirm: your marks, years, subjects, English scores, study plans and contact details.

    Questions

    Student Pool FAQ

    Is it really free?

    Yes. Student Pool is a free service. You don't pay to upload, to get your profile reviewed or to receive your study options.

    When will I hear back?

    Within 24 hours of sending your profile, by email (and by phone or WhatsApp if we need anything). Check your spam folder if you don't see it.

    Which documents should I upload?

    Your 10th and 12th marksheets, any diploma, bachelor's or master's marksheets, transcripts or degree certificates, and your English test result. If you haven't taken an English test yet, tick the box in the English section.

    Do you keep my documents?

    No. Each document is read once to build your profile and then permanently deleted. We never store copies. Only the details you confirm are kept, and you can ask us to delete your profile at any time by replying to our email.

    Is this immigration advice?

    No. Our education consultants help you find suitable programs and colleges. WorkerPool is a data processing company and does not provide immigration advice or representation. For a study permit application, use a licensed immigration consultant (CICC) or a lawyer.
