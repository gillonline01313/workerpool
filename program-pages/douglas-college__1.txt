POST-GRADUATION WORK PERMIT ELIGIBILITY
Graduates may be eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) upon completion of an eligible program at
Douglas College. Eligibility is not guaranteed and subject to the criteria established by Immigration, Refugees
and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

A complete list of Douglas College programs and their descriptions is available at douglascollege.ca/programs.
Each program is assigned a Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) code that accurately reflects its content
and focus.

    •   Programs listed in this document have CIP Codes that align the field of study requirement under IRCC’s
        current PGWP eligibility criteria for college programs.

    •   Douglas College bachelor degree programs are not listed in this document because, according to IRCC’s
        current PGWP eligibility criteria, there are no field of study requirements for graduates who hold a
        bachelor degree.
