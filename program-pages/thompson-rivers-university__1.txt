Programs marked as PGWP eligible meet the field of study requirements for the Post-Graduation Work Permit, allowing graduates to apply for a work permit
upon completion. Graduating from a PGWP-eligible program in Canada may qualify you for a PGWP, but it’s not guaranteed. The PGWP is issued by
Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).
