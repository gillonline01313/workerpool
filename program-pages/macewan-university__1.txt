MacEwan’s Credit Program CIP codes for IRCC purposes
Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada has implemented new eligibility criteria for Post-graduation Work Permits (PGWP). There
are several PGWP eligibility criteria that students must meet in addition to program eligibility.

Field of study requirement CIPs
There are no field of study requirements for university graduates with a bachelor’s degree. This means that all of MacEwan’s bachelor
degrees are PGWP eligible, regardless of subject or major.

Certificate or Diploma programs:

   •   If you submitted your study permit application before November 1, 2024, there are no field of study requirements.
   •   If you submitted your study permit application for a certificate or diploma program on or after November 1, 2024, you must
       graduate from a program with a PGWP eligible field of study Classification of Instructional Program (CIP) code.

MacEwan’s certificate and diploma program CIPs are listed below. To confirm if the program is PGWP eligible, find your program CIP code
and enter it onto the PGWP eligible field of study search tool. If the CIP code appears on the PGWP eligible field of study list, the program
is PGWP eligible.

We recommend students take a screen shot of the program CIP code if it appears on the IRCC PGWP list. Ensure the screenshot shows a
date and time stamp taken immediately before submitting your study permit application and retain that image for your PGWP application.
