We understand that planning your education in Canada as an international student involves several important decisions, one of the most crucial being ensuring your program qualifies for Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) eligibility. This permit can open doors for you to work in Canada after completing your studies. To help you choose the right program, we've compiled a list of KPU programs that align with the eligible CIP codes based on the latest PGWP guidelines from Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Last updated on January 19, 2026.

PGWP Eligible Programs at KPU

DISCLAIMER: Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) Eligibility Enrolment in a PGWP-eligible program does not guarantee PGWP approval by Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Students are responsible for ensuring they meet all IRCC eligibility requirements. PGWP approvals are solely determined by IRCC, and KPU cannot influence or expedite the process. Please also note, that the list of Classification of Instructional Program (CIP) codes, as posted by IRCC is subject to change.

Why Study at KPU?

As a polytechnic university, we're dedicated to ensuring you get a wealth of hands-on, Canadian experience that enhances your education. Dozens of our programs offer multiple opportunities for experiential learning including:

  • Practicums
  • Applied research
  • Field schools
  • Co-operative education
  • On-campus studios and labs

KPU is well connected to industry leaders and professional organizations throughout the region. You’ll have the opportunity to gain real-world experience, while making genuine contributions to the communities you’ll be part of.

Meet Our Students

A KPU student is someone who wants more out of their post-secondary education than just textbooks and lecture halls. If you want to learn through hands-on instruction, gain experience in the real world, and build connections to industry employers, you'll fit right in at KPU.

Whether you have a eye for design, a head for business, or a desire to help others, you can pursue a wide range of disciplines at KPU. From the social sciences to laboratory science to farrier science, KPU offers academic and trades programs, including preparatory, vocational, undergraduate and graduate, that you won't find at a traditional university.
