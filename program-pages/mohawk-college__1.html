Post-Graduation Work Permits (PGWP) Eligible Programs

The Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) allows you to gain Canadian work experience directly related to your field of study, enhancing your resume and industry expertise. We understand that program eligibility can be one of the many reasons you choose to pursue your education with Mohawk College. Choosing the right program is essential, and we’re proud to offer many programs that currently align with Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) eligibility, which may allow you to work in Canada after completing your studies. When you select a program from the list below you open doors to invaluable work experience in Canada, advancing your skills and boosting your career prospects wherever your path may take you.

Review the list below and take the first step toward a rewarding future—whether you aim to make Canada your base for an exciting in-demand career or if you want to apply your skills worldwide!

Update: Program Eligibility as of June 25, 2025

Some program eligibility has changed as result of the IRCC announcement on June 25, 2025. Students who have submitted study permit applications to IRCC on or after June 25, 2025 will have program eligibility status determined as noted below.

Important Information about the Post-Graduation Work Permit  

Please be advised that while Mohawk College is providing information on programs that are eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (“PGWP”) to help support you in making an informed choice about your studies with us, program eligibility requirements are subject to change and at the discretion of the IRCC. We have provided this information based on the latest guidance applicable for the intakes indicated below. However, future program eligibility is not guaranteed. We strongly recommend that applicants consult the official Government of Canada website for the most current PGWP eligibility criteria prior to enrolling. Mohawk College is not liable for any changes made by the government that may affect your programs eligibility for PGWP.
