PGWP-eligible programs

IRCC (Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada) has recently announced changes to the post-graduation work permit (PGWP) eligibility. Based on the study program requirements from IRCC, graduates of the following VCC certificate, diploma and post-degree diploma programs may be eligible for PGWP as the CIP (Classification of Instructional Programs) to program alignment. Bachelor's degree programs are eligible for PGWP without field of study requirements, as per IRCC.

About CIP Codes

CIP codes are determined through a review of a program’s curriculum, learning outcomes, and overall instructional content, rather than solely based on the program name. Educational institutions in British Columbia assign CIP codes according to the program’s primary field of study and report these classifications to the BC Ministry of Post-Secondary Education and Future Skills as part of their institutional reporting processes.
