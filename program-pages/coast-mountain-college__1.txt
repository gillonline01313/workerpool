CMTN: PGWP-eligible Programs for International Applications
Last updated: December 15, 2025
Important Notice: Changes to PGWP Eligibility
Key Dates:
 September 18, 2024
    • Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) announced major updates to the Post-Graduation
         Work Permit Program (PGWPP).
 November 1, 2024
    • Language Requirement: If you submit a Post-Graduation Work permit (PGWP) application on or after
         November 1, 2024, college graduates must meet a language proficiency requirement.
    • Field of Study Requirement: International students who submit a study permit application for a college
         diploma, college certificate, or college degree on or after November 1, 2024, MUST graduate from a
         designated field of study to be eligible for a PGWP.
 June 25, 2025
    • New change effective June 25, 2025:
         If a study permit or PGWP application was submitted before June 25, 2025, this new rule does not apply.
         Eligibility for a PGWP remains after graduation, even if the program is later removed from the approved
         list—provided students do not change their program.
 July 4, 2025 *new IRCC updates
    • IRCC has updated the list of PGWP-eligible programs. Some programs that were removed on June 25,
         2025 are now added back.
    • This updated list of programs remains PGWP-eligible until early 2026. Study permit applications submitted
         between June 25, 2025 and early 2026 for a program listed on the PGWP-eligible list will still meet the
         requirements for a PGWP after program completion.

There have been no new IRCC changes specific to PGWP eligibility announced after July 4, 2025 (such as new
rules, major criteria changes, or additional key dates) that would further affect the Post-Graduation Work Permit
requirements beyond what you already have in your document. The last formal PGWP policy update from IRCC
was the eligible-program list update on July 4, 2025.

Impact on Coast Mountain College (CMTN) Students:
The Classification of Instructional Programs (CIP) is a system used in Canada to categorize post-secondary
education programs by field of study. At CMTN, each program has its own CIP code. Some programs at CMTN are
eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP), while others are NOT. The CIP codes linked to PGWP eligibility
on the IRCC website may change, so always check IRCC’s official website for the latest updates.

Important Reminders:
   • Completing an eligible program does NOT guarantee a PGWP—final approval is at IRCC’s discretion.
   • This document will be updated as new information from IRCC becomes available. However, it is highly
       recommended to check IRCC’s official website for the latest CIP codes and the most up-to-date list of
       PGWP-eligible programs.

This information is current as of December 15, 2025.
