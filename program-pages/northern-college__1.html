Programs Available to International Students

We are thrilled you’d like to pursue your studies with us!

When you are ready to apply, visit OCAS International to submit your documentation so we can review your application.

New international students from all over the world in select programs starting in eligible intakes at Northern College may be eligible to receive an entrance scholarship valued at $2,000 CAD per recipient.

Program Availability Summary

Important: The Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC) has published new PGWP eligibility requirements for international students who have applied for a study permit on or after November 1, 2024. This opportunity for international students allows them to live and work in Canada for up to three years after graduation.

College students who apply for their study permit on or after November 1, 2024 must graduate from a study program linked to certain occupations in long-term shortage to be eligible for the PGWP program.
