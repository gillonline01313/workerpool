Getting to Know Red Deer Polytechnic

Founded in 1964 as Red Deer College. 

Now, as Red Deer Polytechnic:

  • About 10,000 students are enrolled in our programs each year
  • 1,000 international students from 25+ countries (2023/24)
  • 94% of graduates are satisfied with the quality of their education
  • 91% of graduates find employment within six months of graduation

RDP's scenic main campus features world-class facilities, including a variety of spaces to study and socialize.

Why International Students Enjoy Attending Red Deer Polytechnic

  • Offering programs eligible for the Post-Graduation Work Permit Program.
  • Small class sizes in most programs
  • Expert instructors who want students to succeed
  • Focus on hands-on, industry-relevant learning 
  • Prepares graduates for careers
  • Modern and high-quality facilities 
  • Lower cost of living compared to larger Canadian cities

About Red Deer, Alberta

  • Located in central Alberta, midway between the major cities of Edmonton & Calgary on the Queen Elizabeth II (QE II) Highway.
  • We are the third-largest city in Alberta 
  • We have a diverse economy, with 62,224 jobs provided by over 4,000 businesses.
  • Key Industries include Health Care & Social Assistance; Retail; Construction; Oil and Gas; Hospitality; Manufacturing; and Education. 
  • We are home to 100,844 residents (2021)
  • Red Deer Transit provides bus service throughout the city (Routes and Map)
