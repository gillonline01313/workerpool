PGWP-aligned programs

If you're interested in working in Canada after you graduate, please make sure your program aligns with the post-graduation work permit (PGWP) eligibility guidelines from Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

Based on the information currently available, the Sheridan programs listed below meet these requirements, and may allow you to acquire a PGWP after completing your studies.

Note: All bachelor's degree and master's degree programs are PGWP-aligned with no field of study restrictions.
