Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) Eligibility

This page outlines the programs at Cambrian College affected by recent changes to the Immigration, Refugees, Citizenship Canada (IRCC) policies regarding CIP (Classification of Instructional Programs) codes. These regulations specify which programs are eligible for the Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) effective as November 1, 2024.

It is important to note that these regulations, as well as the list of programs below, are subject to change as IRCC updates its criteria. We strongly recommend reviewing this page regularly to ensure you have the most accurate and up-to-date information.

While some programs qualify for PGWP based on CIP codes, all our other programs remain open for enrollment, providing excellent learning opportunities and career pathways.

Have questions?

If you have questions about the regulations, PGWP eligibility, or your program of interest, please contact us. Our team is here to help you navigate these changes and plan your future!
