Ask Us Webinar for International Students

Register to learn about the study permit application process and answer all your questions.

At Seneca Polytechnic, we’re committed to helping you choose a program that supports your journey in Canada and career aspirations.

If you plan to work in Canada after graduation and meet all the eligibility requirements, you may apply for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP), This permit is issued by the Government of Canada through Immigration, Refugees & Citizenship Canada (IRCC).

The list below reflects Seneca programs that are PGWP eligible based on IRCC requirements as of July 4, 2025. As IRCC policies may change, this list will be updated as needed. We recommend checking back regularly.

To confirm if your program of choice is available, please visit the Program Availability List to see which programs are currently open to international applicants.

Have questions?  

Learn more about latest changes by visiting our FAQs or book an appointment with our immigration specialist for advisement. For any additional support, please contact The Service Hub.
