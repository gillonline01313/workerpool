Post-Graduate Work Permit

The post-graduation work permit (PGWP) allows students who have graduated from eligible programs to work in Canada to gain valuable work experience.

IRCC Important Update:

All graduating students applying for post-graduation work permits (PGWP) must meet the Canadian Language Benchmark (CLB) 5 for college programs. It is the student’s responsibility to ensure they meet all PGWP eligibility requirements. We encourage students to become familiar with eligibility policies and the application process by visiting the Immigration, Refugees, and Citizenship Canada website. 

On March 14, 2025, IRCC confirmed that graduates of bachelor’s degree programs are not required to meet the field of study requirements. Graduates of Diploma and Post-Degree-Diploma programs who applied for their study permit on or after November 1, 2024, continue to be required to meet field of study requirements when applying for PGWP.

On July 4, 2025 IRCC updated the list of eligible CIP codes to add back fields of study that were removed on June 25, 2025. Those fields of study that were removed on June 25, 2025 will stay eligible until the list is next updated in early 2026. Please check the list of currently eligible CIP codes here.

All Langara international students and recent graduates with a valid Langara student ID are eligible to receive $20 off the regular IELTS registration fee through MOSAIC. The discount code can be used at all MOSAIC locations.

Students can register on the MOSAIC website.

Disclaimer: Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) Eligibility

Enrolment in a program that is eligible for a Post-Graduation Work Permit (PGWP) at Langara College does not guarantee PGWP approval by IRCC. It is the student’s responsibility to ensure they meet all eligibility requirements as outlined by Immigration, Refugees and Citizenship Canada (IRCC). Final decisions on PGWP applications rest with the immigration officer adjudicating the application. Langara College cannot influence or expedite the processing or approval of any application. List of CIP codes are subject to change by IRCC.
